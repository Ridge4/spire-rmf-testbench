=== The leaf subject did not change ===

After rotation, workload SVIDs are issued by the new CA but still carry
SPIRE's own subject DN:

  MTLS peer=spiffe://spire-rmf.lab/ns/payments/sa/echo-client
       subject=O=SPIRE,C=US

caSubject sets the issuer DN only. The subject on a workload certificate is
set by SPIRE and is not configurable through this value. An organisation that
wants its own name on workload certificates needs the credential composer
(credentialComposer.cel in the chart), not caSubject.

Three layers, three mechanisms:
  trust domain  -> global.spire.trustDomain, effective immediately
  CA subject    -> caSubject, effective only at CA rotation
  leaf subject  -> SPIRE internal; credential composer required to change
